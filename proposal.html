01 · The problem

Three groups lose time every single day.

😟
The one who lost something

Has to walk to the desk, describe the item from memory, and come back again and again to ask "is it here yet?" — often between classes.

🗂️
The Lost & Found desk

Answers the same questions all day, searches a logbook and a box by hand, and has no easy way to tell students what is already there.

🧭
New students & visitors

Don't know where the registrar, a faculty room or "Room 305" is — so they ask around, get lost, and arrive late.

Most lost items are never claimed — not because nobody found them, but because the owner never knew they were found.Hanap QCU closes that gap.

02 · A day at the desk

The same questions, all day long.

Every one of these takes the staff away from real work — and every one of them can be answered by a screen.

"Did anyone turn in a black umbrella?" "I lost my ID yesterday, is it here?" "What time are you open?" "Where exactly is the Lost & Found desk?" "Can I check again tomorrow?" "I found this phone in Room 210 — what do I do?" "Where is the registrar's office?" "What do I bring to claim it?" "Has my water bottle been found?"
03 · The idea

One web app. Two jobs.

No install, no account needed to look — just open the link on any phone or computer.

🔎
Lost & Found, on a map

Every found item gets a photo, a reference code and a pin on the exact spot — building, floor and room. Search by color, brand or place. Students see what's there before walking to the desk.

WORKING TODAY
🧭
Campus Finder

Type "registrar", "CR", "faculty room" or "room 305" and the map flies there — with the floor, the room number, office hours and walking directions.

WORKING TODAY
04 · How it works

Three steps from "I found this" to "it's back."

01
Report in a minute

Pick "I found" or "I lost", describe it, add a photo, then drag the map to pin the exact spot — the building is detected for you. Add floor and room.

02
The desk checks it

The report becomes a QR code. Show it at the desk — staff scan it and it opens on their screen. Nothing to type, nothing to copy into a logbook.

03
Live on the map

One click and it's on the map with a reference code. The owner finds it, reads exactly what to bring, and claims it at the desk.

Nothing goes public until the desk checks it — no spam, no fake posts.

05 · For the one who lost something

Check from your phone — not from the line.

WHAT THEY GET
  • See every found item with a photo, where and when it was found
  • Tap a category bubble — phones, IDs, bags — new items glow
  • Search "blue tumbler" or "library" in one box
  • "Is it yours?" tells them exactly what to bring and the reference code to mention
  • Report a lost item so finders and the desk can look out for it
  • "My reports" keeps track of everything they sent

08 · A map built by everyone

The admin doesn't map the school. The school maps itself.

Any student or teacher can add a place they know — a faculty room, a hidden CR, a new office. The desk or a student moderator approves it with one click. The map grows and stays correct, without one person doing all the work.

📍
Anyone adds

Drop a pin, give it a name, floor and room — even a photo of the door sign.

✅
Quick review

A moderator approves, edits or rejects — like the lost & found reports today.

🗺️
Live for everyone

It appears on the map and in search, marked as "added by the community".

🔁
Kept fresh

"Still correct?" — users flag moved offices, so the map fixes itself.

Helpful to everyone. Useful to no thief.

🔒
Contacts stay private

Names and phone numbers go only to the desk — never shown on the site.

WORKING TODAY
🛂
Claim in person

WORKING TODAY
👀

WORKING TODAY

WORKING TODAY

11 · Costs nothing to run

₱0 a month. No server to maintain.

Hanap QCU runs on free, reliable services the school doesn't have to manage.

₱0
Hosting

Free website hosting on GitHub Pages — fast everywhere.

🗺️ Maps

Free satellite, street and OpenStreetMap campus outline.

🖼️ Photos

Stored as links and resized on the fly — about 30 KB per photo on a phone.

📦 Data

One small file with every item and place, cached on the phone.

🧰 Upkeep

The desk updates everything from one admin page.
